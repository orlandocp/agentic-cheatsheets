# Style rules

- Audience: knows general technology, does not know this topic.
- Language: Spanish by default, or the language agreed at creation. Keep identifiers and product
  names as they are, in code font when they are identifiers.
- Short, direct sentences. Active voice. Plain words.
- One name per thing across the whole sheet.
- Banned:
  - asides set off with dashes (—);
  - "not X, but Y" framing;
  - a colon that introduces a reveal ("The key: …");
  - scare quotes;
  - chained relative clauses ("a script, that Claude writes, that …").
- Check spelling, accents, punctuation and agreement.
- In reports to the user, never claim the sheet is perfect. Say what was checked and what was not.
